\chapter{Full Session Overviews}
\label{ch:session-overview}

The five sessions referenced throughout Chapter~\ref{ch:results} are
shown in full here. Each figure spans the entire recording, with block
shading indicating the protocol phase (Baseline, EDA-Only, EMG-Only,
or Combined EMG+EDA) and vertical lines marking cue onsets. Sessions
1 and 2 show pre- and post-isolation channels for a single modality;
Sessions 3 to 5 show the post-isolation EMG and EDA channels recorded
simultaneously.
